# Seven Deadly Sins: Prisoners of the Sky (Nanatsu no Taizai: Tenkū no Torawarebito)

## Overview
**The Seven Deadly Sins: Prisoners of the Sky** (*Nanatsu no Taizai: Tenkū no Torawarebito*) is a **2018** Japanese animated fantasy adventure film based on Nakaba Suzuki's manga series. Traveling in search of a rare ingredient for the King's birthday, Meliodas and Hawk are transported to the *Sky Palace* above the clouds, where they must defend the winged Celestial race from a resurrected demon clan known as the **Six Knights of Black**.



---

## Main Characters & Sins
* **Meliodas** – *Dragon's Sin of Wrath* (Captain of the Seven Deadly Sins)
* **Elizabeth Liones** – Third Princess of the Kingdom of Liones
* **Hawk** – Captain of the Order of Scraps Disposal
* **Diane** – *Serpent's Sin of Envy*
* **Ban** – *Fox's Sin of Greed*
* **King** – *Grizzly's Sin of Sloth*
* **Gowther** – *Goat's Sin of Lust*
* **Merlin** – *Boar's Sin of Gluttony*
* **Escanor** – *Lion's Sin of Pride*

---

## Top 3 Highlights of the Movie
1. **The Sky Palace Setting** – Explores a floating world inhabited by the Celestial race, expanding the lore of the series.
2. **Solaad and Ellatte** – Introduces memorable movie-original characters who share striking similarities with Meliodas and Elizabeth.
3. **Full Sin Teamwork** – Showcases all seven members of the Seven Deadly Sins fighting together at full strength against the Six Knights of Black.
